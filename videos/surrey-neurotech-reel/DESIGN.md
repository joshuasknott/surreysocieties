# Design System

## Overview

Surrey Neurotech Society uses a scientific, student-accessible identity built around a deep navy canvas, glowing neural imagery, and compact route panels. The homepage opens with a large image-led brain hero, warm ivory and gold editorial typography, cyan signal accents, and thin bordered modules for actions and activity cards. The page alternates between dark signal-atlas sections and pale blue-white community sections, making the science feel inviting rather than clinical. The overall feel is intelligent, responsible, curious, and human.

## Colors

- **Primary Deep Surface**: `#031426` - hero and dark scientific canvas.
- **Footer Deep Surface**: `#02112B` - deepest navy for final brand closure.
- **Dark Panel Surface**: `#021227` - route cards and compact action panels.
- **Primary Content on Dark**: `#FFF8E8` - warm ivory hero and section text.
- **Muted Content on Dark**: `#CBD8DF` - supporting copy on navy.
- **Primary Ink on Light**: `#07162D` - headings and body text on pale sections.
- **Gold Accent**: `#D4AF37` - primary CTA fills, dots, and emphasis.
- **Soft Gold Accent**: `#F1DC91` - small headings and labels on dark surfaces.
- **Cyan Signal Accent**: `#00B4D8` - icons, outlines, signal lines, and secondary CTAs.
- **Teal Accent**: `#0B5F76` - light-section labels and outlines.
- **Light Wash**: `#E7EEF3` - pale activity section base.
- **Light Paper**: `#F4F7F9` - bright supporting surface.

## Typography

- **Display Serif**: Fraunces (400, 650, 700, 750, 850). Used for large emotional statements such as "Neurotech, for good." and "Explore. Discuss. Create. Together." Heavy weights create an editorial, academic warmth.
- **Primary Sans**: Inter (400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 850, 900). Used for navigation, body copy, labels, CTAs, and compact panel content. Uppercase labels use heavy weights and modest tracking.
- **Hierarchy**: Hero display type is very large and tight, section headings remain bold and editorial, body copy stays compact and readable, and small route labels are uppercase with gold/cyan contrast.

## Elevation

Depth comes from image layering, thin borders, translucent navy panels, and localized glow rather than heavy shadows. Dark modules use 1px borders in gold, cyan, ivory, or blue-tinted transparency. The hero relies on a protected left reading zone over a luminous brain image, while pale sections use bordered list modules and image thumbnails with small radii.

## Components

- **Image-Led Neural Hero**: Full-width dark hero with a luminous brain and network-lines image, large Fraunces headline, gold rule, and compact CTA cluster.
- **Signal Route Cards**: Three bordered dark cards for Learn, Meet, Build responsibly; each uses small numeric markers, gold labels, and cyan arrows.
- **Event Strip**: A compact dark horizontal panel with a circular "Now" marker, status label, and events CTA.
- **Pathway Signal Track**: Six circular icon markers connected by a dashed gold line, showing Psychology, Computing, Bioscience, Design, Ethics, and Curious beginners.
- **Activity Ledger**: Light-section bordered list of Talks & Events, Workshops, Inter-University Collaborations, and Socials & Community, with small circular numbers and student image thumbnails.
- **Action Rail**: Dark three-column rail for Events, Join, and Committee, matching the route-card logic.
- **Footer Identity Block**: Deep navy footer with logo, established date, a gold rule, and simple Explore/Connect link columns.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#031426` and `#02112B` as the main cinematic surfaces.
- Use `#D4AF37` and `#F1DC91` for responsible, optimistic emphasis.
- Use `#00B4D8` as the signal color for neural lines, icons, outlines, and pulses.
- Keep layouts structured with thin borders, rows, route cards, and measured spacing.
- Combine scientific visuals with student-community imagery so the tone stays human.

### Don'ts

- Do not make the video feel like a cold medical device ad.
- Do not replace the brand palette with generic purple, neon green, or black-and-white tech styling.
- Do not use heavy glassmorphism or large rounded SaaS cards; the site uses compact 6-8px radii and border-led panels.
- Do not over-explain BCI or AI with intimidating jargon; keep the language open and approachable.
- Do not let text sit on a flat empty background; each frame needs signal lines, image texture, or structured panels.
