# Design System

## Overview

Surrey Business Society uses an editorial student-professional identity: dark ink sections, warm paper sections, gold action states, and burgundy join moments. The homepage alternates between full-bleed desk imagery and clean, line-divided content sections, so it feels credible without becoming corporate stock. The typography pairs large Playfair Display statements with compact DM Sans labels and body copy. The visual tone is practical, opportunity-led, and grounded in student community.

## Colors

- **Ink Surface**: `#071A2D` — main dark section and navigation background.
- **Ink Deep**: `#051525` — footer and darkest depth layer.
- **Ink Soft**: `#102B46` — supporting dark panels and shaded details.
- **Paper Surface**: `#F5F0E7` — warm cream page sections.
- **Paper Light**: `#FFFCF6` — lightest surface and primary light text.
- **On-Dark Text**: `#FFFAF1` — warm white typography over imagery and ink.
- **Gold Accent**: `#D7A845` — CTAs, section labels, numbering, active nav underline.
- **Gold Light**: `#EDC76E` — hover/highlight glow.
- **Burgundy CTA**: `#861F35` — final join band and text-link accent.
- **Burgundy Dark**: `#641426` — deeper CTA accent.
- **Muted Text**: `#526172` — secondary copy on paper.

## Typography

- **Display Serif**: Playfair Display, 600-800. Used for large hero claims, section headings, and quote-like moments. It has an editorial, institutional confidence.
- **Sans Serif**: DM Sans, 400-800. Used for body copy, CTAs, labels, and compact supporting information.
- **Extracted Support Font**: Inter, 400-900. Present in capture metadata but secondary to the visible Business identity.
- **Hierarchy**: hero headline around 92px on desktop; section titles around 50-64px; labels are small, uppercase, bold, and tracked.

## Elevation

Depth comes from image overlays, subtle line divisions, and layered warm/dark surfaces rather than floating shadows. The site uses narrow dividers, full-width bands, desaturated editorial images, and dark overlay gradients to keep text readable. Buttons are flat rectangular gold blocks with confident uppercase labels.

## Components

- **Editorial Hero**: full-bleed desk image, dark overlay, Playfair headline, gold primary CTA, outline secondary CTA.
- **Fact Row**: three dark columns with gold serif facts and compact uppercase labels.
- **Line-Divided Reason Blocks**: numbered serif cards separated by thin vertical rules on paper.
- **Ink Focus Section**: dark navy band with four numbered practical routes: Careers, Enterprise, Networking, Skills.
- **Updates Split**: paper section with Upcoming events and The committee side by side.
- **Image Break**: full-width practice image with dark overlay and large quote-style type.
- **Burgundy Join Band**: final conversion band with Playfair heading and gold CTA.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#071A2D`, `#F5F0E7`, `#D7A845`, and `#861F35` as the core palette.
- Keep the message practical: careers, enterprise, networking, confidence, skills, events, conversations, joining.
- Use Playfair Display for thesis lines and DM Sans for proof, labels, and CTAs.
- Use thin rules, numbered lists, editorial crops, and warm dark overlays.
- Let imagery feel like student planning, conversation, direction, and next steps.

### Don'ts

- Do not use generic corporate stock-video scenes, glass offices, handshakes in boardrooms, or fake business metrics.
- Do not introduce neon, purple-blue gradients, glossy SaaS dashboards, or startup cliché styling.
- Do not make the video text-only for more than two consecutive beats.
- Do not soften the society into vague inspiration; keep each claim useful and concrete.
